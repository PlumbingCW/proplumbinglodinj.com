Main Street and the Saddle River

Main Street is the spine of this part of Lodi, and the Saddle River crosses the borough close to it. Public news reports show why basements and low ground matter here. On January 10, 2024, CBS New York reported that the river covered Main Street from Sydney to Kennedy, and that schools and several businesses closed for the day.

Two months later, on March 10, 2024, CBS New York reported that a section of Main Street near the Route 46 exit was closed after the Saddle River overflowed. After the remnants of Hurricane Ida in September 2021, CBS New York reported that sections of the city sat under several feet of water.

These reports describe street flooding. They do not say which basements took on water, and we do not claim that any particular building has a problem.

What river water means for your plumbing

A sump pump moves groundwater out of a basement pit, and a battery backup keeps it running when the power fails. A pump cannot stop a river from rising over the street. Our sump pump installation and repair page explains what a pump can and cannot do, and basement waterproofing covers interior drains and backwater valves.

If sewage or gray water comes up through a floor drain while it is raining, tell the plumber. The cause can be your own line or the shared municipal system, and the fix depends on which one it is. Our drain cleaning page and sewer line repair page show how that is checked.

Mixed buildings and shared lines

Downtown blocks often combine shops, offices and apartments in the same structure. NeighborhoodScout's Borough Center profile for Lodi estimates that about half of the residential real estate in its census tract is in two, three or four unit buildings. That is an aggregator estimate and not a borough record.

As general plumbing information, units in one building can share a drain stack, a water main or a water heater. A clog or a leak in one unit can then reach another. Tell us how many units the building has and whether the shutoff controls one unit or several. Businesses on Main Street can read our commercial plumbing page.

Water, permits and code

Lodi's tap water comes from the Passaic Valley Water Commission, and its 2026 water quality report for Lodi lists hardness between 106 and 242 ppm at the Little Falls plant. Hard water leaves scale in water heaters and faucets, which is why water heater repair calls are common in towns on this supply.

The borough's Building Department says it conducts plumbing subcode inspections, and it lists a phone number of 973-859-7410. Ask any plumber whether your job needs a permit before work starts.

Downtown Lodi plumbing questions

Do you cover all of Downtown Lodi?

We confirm coverage for your street when you call. Give the address and the nearest cross street, and we tell you whether we can help.

Why does my basement floor drain back up when it rains near Main Street?

Heavy rain can overload the sewer system or raise groundwater. It can also point to a blockage in your own line. The plumber checks the line first, then explains whether a sump pump or a backwater valve makes sense.

Will a sump pump keep river water out of my basement?

A sump pump removes groundwater from a pit. It does not stop water that enters from a river overflowing a street. A plumber can tell you what a pump will cover at your address.
