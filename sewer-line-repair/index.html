Sewer line repair fixes the pipe that carries waste from the house to the municipal sewer connection. It covers a cracked section, a joint that roots have pushed open, a sagging belly that holds water and a full collapse. What to do first when sewage backs up

The first minutes matter for health and for damage:

  • Stop using sinks, tubs, toilets and appliances that drain.
  • Keep people and pets out of the affected room. Sewage carries bacteria.
  • Turn off power to the area only if you can reach the breaker without stepping in water.
  • Open windows for ventilation if sewer gas is strong.
  • Take photos of the damage for your insurance claim.

Do not pour chemicals into a backed-up drain. They rarely help with a collapsed or rooted line and make the repair more dangerous for the crew.

Repair, relining or replacement

Three methods cover almost every sewer problem, and the right one depends on the pipe's condition and what is above it.

Sewer repair methods
MethodBest forTrade-off.
Spot repairOne broken or offset sectionNeeds digging at that spot.
Trenchless liningCracks, root entry and small gaps in an otherwise aligned pipeCannot fix a collapse or a deep sag.
Full replacementCollapsed, crushed or badly offset pipeMore digging, higher cost.

Pipe lining sends a resin-soaked liner into the old pipe and cures it into a new inner pipe. It avoids digging up the yard and driveway, which matters where a line runs under pavement or a finished patio. Lining needs a pipe that is still open and roughly straight, so a camera check decides whether it is possible.

How the problem gets diagnosed

  1. Clear the line enough for the camera to pass, using a cable or a jet.
  2. Run a sewer camera from the clean-out toward the street, recording the pipe.
  3. Mark the location and depth of any break, sag or root intrusion.
  4. Review the recording with you, so you see the same thing the plumber sees.
  5. Present repair options with a clear price for each.

The recording is yours to keep, which is useful for insurance and for comparing quotes. Our sewer inspection page covers the camera work in more detail.

Why Saddle River flooding matters for sewers

Lodi sits along the Saddle River, and its low areas flood in heavy rain. When the ground around a buried pipe is saturated again and again, soil shifts and joints can move. Flood events such as the remnants of Hurricane Ida in September 2021, when CBS New York reported sections of the city under several feet of water, put repeated stress on pipe and soil.

That does not mean every Lodi sewer line is damaged. It does mean that a line which backs up after storms is worth a camera look and not just another cable run. A basement drain that fills only during heavy rain may be a municipal system problem, not a house problem, so we help you tell the difference.

Who is responsible for which section

In general the property owner maintains the lateral that runs from the house to the point where it joins the municipal main, and the borough or its sewer authority maintains the main. The exact dividing line varies, and the Lodi borough website links the Passaic Valley Sewerage Commission among its outside agencies.

Before a dig, confirm the boundary with the borough's Building Department or Public Works, and we help you with the questions to ask.

Sewer line repair questions

Do you handle sewer emergencies at night or on weekends in Lodi?

Yes. Our line is open 24/7. A sewage backup is a same-day call, and the plumber states the price before starting work.

How do I know if my sewer line is broken?

Warning signs include repeated backups after cleaning, gurgling at several fixtures, a sewage smell in the yard and soggy ground near the line. A camera inspection confirms it.

Is trenchless lining as good as replacement?

It is a legitimate repair for a pipe that is cracked or rooted but still aligned. It does not fix a collapse or a sag, and the camera tells us which one you have.

Will my homeowners insurance cover it?

Policies vary, and many exclude sewer line wear. Call your insurer, and keep the camera recording and invoice for your claim.

How long does a repair take?

Lining and spot repairs often take a day. Full replacement can take longer depending on length, depth and permits.
