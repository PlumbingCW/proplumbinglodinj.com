Privacy Policy.

How we handle calls, recordings and website data.

This policy explains what information Lodi Pro Plumbing collects when you call us or visit this website, and how it is used. It was last updated on October 15, 2026.

Call recording notice

Calls to (973) 488-5410 may be recorded or monitored for quality, training and dispute resolution. By staying on the line after the notice, you agree to that recording. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers and end the call.

Information we collect

When you call, we may collect your phone number, your name if you give it, your service address, a description of the problem and the recording of the call. We use this to arrange service, give you a price, schedule work and follow up on the job.

This website has no forms, accounts or comment sections. Our hosting provider and content delivery network may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, which is standard for any website and is used for security and performance.

How information is used and shared

We use call information to provide plumbing service and to improve how we answer the phone. We share it with the plumbers and staff who need it to complete your job, and with service providers who handle our phone system and website hosting. We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.

Cookies

The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Embedded Google Maps may set cookies under Google's own policy.

How long we keep information

Call recordings and job records are kept for as long as needed for service, warranty, tax and legal purposes, and then deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to contact@proplumbinglodinj.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to confirm your identity first.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
