Overflows, running water, base leaks and weak flushes fixed, plus new toilet installation when replacement is the better call.

A toilet that is overflowing needs fast action, and the first move is easy: reach behind the bowl and turn the small oval valve clockwise to stop the water. Lodi Pro Plumbing repairs toilets 24/7 in Lodi, NJ, and an overflow or a leak at the base is treated as a same-day call.

If water is still rising, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop the flow into the bowl.

Toilet repair covers three families of problem: a clog or overflow, a running or weak-flushing toilet caused by tank parts, and a leak at the base or supply line. Most are small repairs. Replacement makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the toilet is badly stained and worn, or repairs keep returning.

Clogged and overflowing toilets

A single clogged toilet usually has a cause you can see: too much paper, a wipe or a child's toy. A closet auger, which has a protected end, clears most of them without scratching the porcelain. If the toilet clears, but a nearby tub or floor drain gurgles, the problem is not the toilet. It is the main line, and the sewer line cleaning page applies.

Do not keep flushing a toilet that is not clearing. Each flush adds water to a bowl that cannot drain, and a second overflow makes a bigger cleanup. Do not use chemical cleaners in a clogged toilet, because they leave caustic water in the bowl and hurt the person who has to plunge it.

Running, ghost-flushing and weak flushes

A toilet that runs or refills on its own is wasting water. The usual causes sit inside the tank:

  • A flapper that no longer seals, letting water leak into the bowl.
  • A fill valve that does not shut off at the right water level.
  • A chain that is too tight or too loose.
  • A float set too high so water spills into the overflow tube.
  • Scale on the flush valve seat that stops a seal from forming.

Lodi's water hardness plays a part here. The Passaic Valley Water Commission's 2026 report lists hardness from 106 to 242 ppm at its Little Falls plant. Mineral deposits build up on flapper seats, fill valve screens and the rim jets of the bowl, and they cause slow leaks and weak flushes. A new flapper and a cleaned seat often fix both.

A wax ring leak that goes unnoticed can rot the subfloor. If the floor near the toilet feels soft, call quickly, and we check the damage when we pull the toilet.

Installing a new toilet

Installation covers more than setting a bowl on the flange. The plumber:

  1. Shuts off water, drains the tank and removes the old toilet.
  2. Inspects the closet flange and the subfloor for damage and repairs the flange if needed.
  3. Sets a new wax or foam seal and the new toilet, and tightens the bolts evenly.
  4. Connects a new supply line and valve.
  5. Tests the flush, checks for leaks and caulks the base if the finish calls for it.

Measure the rough-in distance from the wall to the center of the drain flange before you buy, usually 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. A toilet that does not match the rough-in will not fit. We can help you pick one that does.

When a running toilet shows up on the water bill

A running toilet can waste a lot of water and shows up as a high bill. The meter test on our leak detection page shows whether the water is leaking through the toilet or somewhere else. A food coloring test also works. Put a few drops in the tank, wait fifteen minutes without flushing and look at the bowl. If color appears, the flapper leaks.

Toilet questions

Do you repair toilet emergencies at night or on weekends in Lodi?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. An overflow or a leak at the base is a same-day call, and the plumber gives you a clear price before work starts.

Should I repair or replace an old toilet?

Repair is the better choice when the porcelain is sound and the fault is a part such as a flapper or fill valve. Replace when the bowl is cracked, the flush is weak and parts do not help or repairs keep returning.

Why does my toilet keep clogging?

Frequent clogs point to a weak flush, a partial blockage in the trap, or a problem further down the line. If other fixtures also slow down, call about the main line.

Can you install a toilet I bought myself?

Yes. Measure your rough-in first so the toilet fits. We check the flange and supply line when we install it.

Is a toilet leak at the base an emergency?

It is worth fixing quickly, because slow leaks rot subfloors. If water is flowing freely, shut off the valve and call.
