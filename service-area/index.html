Plumber Service Area Around Lodi, NJ.

Crews leave our Harrison Avenue office for Lodi and the five closest Bergen County towns, with ZIP codes and water suppliers listed for each.

Call (973) 488-5410.

Lodi Pro Plumbing is based at 256 Harrison Ave in Lodi, ZIP 07644. The borough covers 2.29 square miles and borders eight other municipalities: Garfield, Hackensack, Hasbrouck Heights, Maywood, Rochelle Park, Saddle Brook, South Hackensack and Wood-Ridge. Five of those towns have their own pages on this site because they are the closest places we work most often, and each page covers that town's streets, utility and common plumbing problems.

Distance matters in an emergency, so we kept the list short. Real drive times depend on Route 17, the Saddle River crossings and traffic, so we confirm coverage when you call and never promise an arrival time we cannot keep.

Towns with their own pages

  • Hackensack, ZIP 07601: the Bergen County seat and the largest neighbor, with downtown Main Street and the hospital district.
  • Saddle Brook, ZIP 07663: the township north of Lodi, named for the Saddle River that also floods Lodi.
  • Hasbrouck Heights, ZIP 07604: the borough to the southeast, along Route 17 and next to Teterboro Airport.
  • Maywood, ZIP 07607: the small borough to the northeast with a historic railroad station and Memorial Park.
  • Wood-Ridge, ZIP 07075: the borough to the south, a borough that grew 32.9 percent between the 2010 and 2020 censuses.

Who supplies the water in each town

Knowing your water supplier helps when a pressure or hardness problem is not a plumbing fault. Lodi buys from the Passaic Valley Water Commission, which serves the borough under public water system ID NJ0231001. Hackensack, Hasbrouck Heights, Maywood and Wood-Ridge appear on the New Jersey WaterCheck list of towns served by Veolia Water New Jersey's Hackensack system, ID NJ0238001.

Saddle Brook runs its own Water Department, which buys all its water from other systems, including Veolia Hackensack.

What the shared geography means for plumbing

Lodi and Saddle Brook share the Saddle River, and both towns took on basement water when the remnants of Hurricane Ida passed through in September 2021, according to CBS New York. One Saddle Brook resident told the station that water reached his basement for the first time in 15 years. That history is why sump pump service and basement waterproofing get so much attention on our pages for those two towns.

Route 17 runs through Hasbrouck Heights, Maywood and Wood-Ridge as well as Lodi, so shops, restaurants and homes sit close together along the same corridor. Our commercial plumbing and drain cleaning pages cover the problems that follow from that mix.
