What winter does to Maywood plumbing

A cold snap hits pipes in unheated spaces first: garages, crawl spaces, exterior walls and basements near leaky windows. A frozen pipe can split without any sign until the thaw, when water pours from the break. Our burst pipe repair page has a step-by-step list for shutting off water, thawing safely and calling for help.

Winter is also when water heaters fail, because they run longer and a tired element or thermocouple gives out under load. A no hot water call on a freezing night is a same-day job. Start with water heater repair, and ask about replacement if the tank is leaking.

Spring rain, Route 17 and drains

Wet springs push groundwater up around foundations and test the drains that carry storm water away. Maywood sits next to Route 17, the most significant highway through the borough, and Maywood Avenue is its main local road. Homes and shops along those routes share the same weather and the same overloaded storm drains.

If the lowest drain in your house bubbles during a storm, or a basement floor drain fills when the street is wet, call us. The drain cleaning page explains how we separate a clog in your line from a problem in the municipal system.

Places that define the borough

Maywood's identity comes from a few real places. The Maywood Station Museum, the restored railroad station, was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2003. The Oldis-Brinckerhoff House and the Romine-Van Voorhis House, both on Maywood Avenue, were listed in 1983, and the Romine-Van Voorhis house dates to 1780. Memorial Park on Grant Avenue, across from Memorial School, has ball fields, a dog park and playgrounds.

Plumbers care about these places for a practical reason. Old buildings and historic houses have old pipe, and old pipe can need special care. If you own a house with historic fabric, tell the plumber when you call so the crew can plan the access and the repair method.
