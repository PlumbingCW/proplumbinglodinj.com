Plumbing Services in Lodi, NJ.

Every repair, replacement and installation we handle for Lodi homes and small businesses, grouped by the problem you are trying to fix.

Call (973) 488-5410.

Most plumbing calls fall into a handful of families: water you cannot get rid of, water you cannot stop, water that is the wrong temperature, and fixtures that need to be installed or swapped. This index groups our pages by those families so you can find the right one fast.

If you are not sure what is wrong, call (973) 488-5410. A plumber will ask a few questions, tell you which page fits and explain what the visit involves. The line is open 24/7, and urgent problems are handled the same day.

Drains and sewers

Backups and slow drains start with drain cleaning. Repeat clogs and grease buildup call for hydro jetting. When the problem sits in the line under the yard, see sewer line cleaning, sewer camera inspection and sewer line repair.

Leaks and pipes

Hidden water is the costly kind. Leak detection finds it without opening walls at random. Burst pipe repair stops active flooding, water service line repair covers the pipe from the street to the house, and repiping replaces supply lines that keep failing.

Hot water

A tank that leaks, a pilot that will not stay lit or a shower that runs cold sends most people to water heater repair. Older units move to water heater replacement, new ones go in through water heater installation, and on-demand units have their own page at tankless water heater service. Boiler repair covers hot water heating systems.

Fixtures and installations

Toilet repair and installation, faucet repair and garbage disposal service cover the fixtures you touch every day. Plumbing installation handles new lines and new fixtures for remodels.

Water in the basement

Lodi sits along the Saddle River, and low areas flood in heavy rain. Sump pump installation and repair and basement waterproofing deal with water that comes from outside. Businesses can start at commercial plumbing.

How to choose the right page

All plumbing work follows the New Jersey plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, which adopts the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code.

Start with what the water is doing. If it is standing or backing up, read the drain and sewer pages. If it is leaking or spraying, read the leak and burst pipe pages. If the water is the wrong temperature, read the water heater and boiler pages. If you are remodeling, start with plumbing installation and the fixture pages.

Each page explains what the visit involves, what changes the price and what to do while you wait. The price is stated before work starts, and the plumber explains the cause first.

Every plumbing service we run

Drain Cleaning

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, a leaking tank or a pilot that keeps going out, diagnosed and repaired in one visit when parts allow.

Water Heater Replacement

A tank that has failed or leaks is removed and replaced with a correctly sized new heater, hauled away the same visit.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs for remodels, fuel switches and efficiency upgrades, set up so the heater is easy to service later.

Tankless Water Heater

Install, repair and descale on-demand heaters, with gas line, venting and hard water accounted for.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water scours grease, scale, silt and roots from drain and sewer lines that keep clogging.

Leak Detection

Hidden leaks pinpointed with acoustic, thermal and pressure testing, so only the damaged spot is opened up.

Sewer Line Repair

Spot repairs, trenchless lining and full replacement for broken, rooted or collapsed sewer lines.

Sewer Camera Inspection

A camera travels the sewer line, records cracks, roots and sags, and gives you footage you can keep.

Sewer Line Cleaning
