When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most slow drains can wait for a morning appointment. These cannot:

  • Water or sewage is coming up through a tub, shower or floor drain.
  • Several fixtures are slow or gurgling at the same time, which points to the main line.
  • A toilet overflows when you flush and the plunger does nothing.
  • The blocked drain sits in a kitchen or laundry room that serves a business or a rental unit.
  • Wastewater is reaching a finished basement or any wall with electrical outlets.

For any of these, we treat the call as a same-day job and send a plumber as soon as one is free, including nights and weekends. Shut off the water to the affected fixture if it has its own valve. If wastewater is near outlets or the electrical panel, keep everyone out of that room.

Why drains block in Lodi homes

Lodi fits 10,414 housing units into 2.29 square miles, according to the 2020 census. That is a lot of households feeding the same neighborhood sewers. Kitchen grease, wipes and hair do most of the damage inside the house, and tree roots do most of it between the house and the street.

Lodi's water also plays a part. The Passaic Valley Water Commission reports hardness between 106 and 242 ppm at its Little Falls plant. Water in that range leaves mineral scale on pipe walls and in P-traps, and scale gives grease and soap a rough surface to cling to. A drain that clogs every few months is often scaled and coated, not just blocked once.

How the blockage is found and cleared

The plumber starts by asking which fixtures are affected, because that points to where the blockage sits. One slow sink means a local clog. A tub, a toilet and a floor drain all slow together means the problem is downstream, at the main line.

  1. Check the fixture, the trap and the vent so the clog is not a venting fault.
  2. Run a hand auger for sinks, tubs and toilets. It reaches the first few feet and rarely damages the pipe.
  3. Run a cable machine with the right cutting head for longer runs and main lines.
  4. Use a drain camera when the clog returns or the cable stops at the same point twice.
  5. Flush the line, run water for several minutes and confirm that every fixture drains at normal speed.

When grease or scale is the cause, a cable only punches a hole through the buildup. Hydro jetting scours the pipe wall and is the better fit for repeat clogs. A line that keeps failing after cleaning may need a camera inspection to look for a break or a sag.

Signs the main line is the problem

A main line clog changes how the whole house behaves. Look for these:

  • A toilet gurgles when the washing machine drains.
  • A basement floor drain bubbles when an upstairs tub empties.
  • Two or more fixtures on different floors drain slowly.
  • Water backs up in the lowest fixture in the house first.

Those symptoms call for a larger machine than a homeowner rents, and the cleaning is usually followed by a look at the line. Our sewer line cleaning page covers that work in detail.

Keeping drains clear between visits

Small habits stretch the time between clogs:

  • Pour cooled grease into a can, not down the sink.
  • Put a hair catcher in every tub and shower.
  • Flush toilet paper only. Wipes labeled flushable still clog lines.
  • Run hot water for a minute after washing greasy dishes.
  • Treat a slow drain early, before it stops completely.

Drains and heavy rain in Lodi

Lodi sits along the Saddle River, and parts of the borough flood in heavy rain. During the remnants of Hurricane Ida in September 2021, CBS New York reported that sections of the city were under several feet of water. In March 2024 the river crested overnight and covered Main and Sydney streets.

Storm water backing up through a basement drain is a different problem from a grease clog. If the lowest drain in the house fills while the street is flooded, stay out of the basement and call us for the plumbing side. The borough's website lists a Stormwater Management page for street flooding.

Drain cleaning questions

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Lodi?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and a drain that is backing up into the house is treated as a same-day call at any hour. The plumber gives you a clear price before any work starts, at any hour.

How long does it take to clear a drain?

A sink or tub clog usually takes under an hour once the plumber is on site. A main line with roots or heavy grease takes longer, and the plumber tells you after the first pass whether jetting or a camera check is needed.

Is chemical drain cleaner safe to use first?

It is better to skip it. Chemical openers rarely clear a full blockage, they can damage older pipe and traps, and they leave caustic liquid standing in the line. Tell the plumber if you used one so the crew can wear the right protection.

Why does the same drain keep clogging?

A repeat clog usually means buildup on the pipe wall, a sag in the line or roots entering a joint. Cabling opens a path, but jetting or a camera check finds and fixes the real cause.

Can you clean a drain in a rental unit or a shop?

Yes. We clear drains in houses, apartments and small commercial spaces along Route 17 and Main Street. See commercial plumbing for business service.
