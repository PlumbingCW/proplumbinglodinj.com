A plumbing emergency is any leak, backup or loss of water that is getting worse while you watch. If you are standing in water, hearing a pipe hiss in a wall or smelling sewage, you need an emergency plumber in Lodi, NJ now and not tomorrow. Lodi Pro Plumbing keeps the line open 24/7, so a person picks up at midnight just as at noon.

Start by shutting off what you can. The main water shutoff is usually near the water meter in the basement or utility room, and turning it clockwise stops water to the whole house. Each toilet, sink and water heater also has its own valve.

If water is near outlets, an appliance or the panel, stay out of the room and turn off power only if you can reach the breaker without standing in water.

What counts as an emergency plumber call

We treat these as same-day jobs, and we answer them at night, on weekends and on holidays:

  • A burst or leaking pipe, or water coming through a ceiling or wall.
  • A sewer backup, or a toilet and tub that overflow together.
  • A water heater that is leaking, flooding a garage or basement, or gives no hot water in winter.
  • A sump pump that has stopped while the basement is taking on water.
  • A smell of rotten eggs from a gas water heater, which means leaving the building and calling the gas utility first.
  • A water meter that keeps running after every fixture is shut off.

Smaller jobs get booked for the next open slot. A dripping faucet, a running toilet or a slow shower drain does not need a midnight visit, though we will still take the call and set a time that suits you.

Water in Lodi and what it does to your plumbing

Lodi's tap water comes from the Passaic Valley Water Commission. Its 2026 water quality report lists hardness between 106 and 242 ppm at the Little Falls plant, which blends in softer Wanaque water for most customers. Water in that range leaves scale inside water heaters, on faucet aerators and in drain traps.

A tank heater that rumbles or takes longer to heat is often fighting a layer of scale. Water heater repair and drain cleaning are the two services where this shows up most.

PVWC also tracks service line materials and publishes the inventory online. If you are not sure who owns the pipe between the street and your meter, the utility's customer service line at 973-340-4300 can tell you, and we can help with the part on your side of the meter. Emergency plumber questions

Are you available 24/7 as an emergency plumber in Lodi?

Yes. The phone line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. When you call, you reach a person who can take your address, ask what is happening and tell you what to shut off while a plumber is assigned.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Shut off the water at the fixture or at the main valve, move valuables out of the wet area and open drawers or cabinets that hold water. Do not use chemical drain cleaners, and stay out of rooms where water meets electrical outlets.

Do you give a price before starting work?

Yes. The plumber diagnoses the problem, explains the options and gives you a clear price before work starts. If the job changes once a wall is open, you hear about it before more work is done.
