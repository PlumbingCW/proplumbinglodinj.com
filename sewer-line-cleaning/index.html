Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Lodi, NJ.

Large cable machines and jetters clear roots, grease and blockages from the main sewer line between your house and the street.

The main sewer line is the single pipe that carries everything out of the house. When it clogs, the lowest drain, usually a basement floor drain or a first-floor tub, fills first and every other fixture slows or gurgles. Lodi Pro Plumbing cleans main sewer lines 24/7 in Lodi, NJ, and a main line backup is treated as a same-day emergency.

Stop using water until the plumber arrives, because the wastewater has nowhere to go but into your house.

Sewer line cleaning uses a larger cable machine than a sink drain needs, with cutting heads that clear roots and grease, or a hydro jet that scours the pipe wall. It restores flow. It does not repair broken pipe, so after the line is open the plumber checks why it clogged.

Signs the main line is blocked

A blocked main line shows up in a pattern:

  • The lowest fixture in the house backs up first.
  • A toilet gurgles or bubbles when a sink drains or the washer runs.
  • Several fixtures on different floors slow down together.
  • Water in a basement floor drain rises when someone upstairs flushes.
  • A sewage smell appears in the basement or yard.

One slow sink means a local clog and is handled by drain cleaning. Multiple fixtures slowing together means the problem is downstream, and this page applies.

The right cleaning method depends on the cause. A cable with a root cutter handles roots. Grease needs a jet. A pipe collapse needs neither, and the plumber stops and explains what the camera shows.

How a cleaning visit works

  1. The plumber locates the clean-out, typically in the basement near the foundation wall, or sets up through a toilet or roof vent when there is none.
  2. A cable machine with the right cutter is run toward the street until the blockage clears.
  3. The plumber pulls out the debris and notes what it is, which hints at the cause.
  4. A camera pass confirms the line is open and shows any damage.
  5. Water runs through all fixtures to confirm normal flow.

We explain what came out of the pipe and what that means for the next step. Roots call for a repair plan. Grease calls for habit changes or scheduled jetting.

Heavy rain and main line backups in Lodi

Not every main line backup is a clog. During intense rain, storm water can overload sewers and push water back through low drains. Lodi's low areas along the Saddle River flood in major storms, and the borough's website lists a Stormwater Management page under its Public Works department.

If the street is flooded and your basement drain fills at the same time, tell the plumber, because cleaning your line will not fix a municipal overflow.

If the backup starts when it is dry outside, the cause is almost certainly in your line, and cleaning is the right move. In both cases keep people out of the affected area and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Preventing the next backup

  • Do not flush wipes, paper towels or feminine products.
  • Pour kitchen grease into a can and discard it with the trash.
  • Keep trees and large shrubs away from the sewer line path if you are planting.
  • Schedule a camera check if the line has clogged more than once in a year.
  • Know where your clean-out is and keep it accessible.

Sewer line cleaning questions

Do you clean main sewer lines at night or on weekends in Lodi?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, and a main line backup is a same-day call. The plumber gives you a clear price before beginning work.

How often should a main line be cleaned?

Only when there is a problem, unless roots or grease are a known issue. A line that has clogged twice in a year may benefit from scheduled cleaning.

Will cleaning fix a broken pipe?

No. Cleaning restores flow. A camera inspection shows whether the pipe needs repair. See sewer line repair.

Can roots grow back after cutting?

Yes, if the pipe still has a gap or joint they can enter. Repair or lining stops them, and cutting only delays them.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Someone needs to give access to the clean-out and the basement. The plumber will tell you what to expect.
