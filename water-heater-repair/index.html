Water heater repair means finding the failed part and fixing it, whether that is a thermostat, a heating element, a thermocouple, a gas control valve or a dip tube. Many failures are cheap to fix. A leak from the tank body is the exception, because a tank that has rusted through cannot be patched.

First steps when the heater is leaking

Where the water comes from decides what happens next:

  • Water at the top fittings or the relief valve pipe often means a loose connection or a failing valve, and the tank may be fine.
  • Water seeping from the bottom drain valve is usually a worn valve that costs little to replace.
  • Water pooling under the tank with a wet jacket points to a leak in the tank itself.

For a gas heater, never relight a pilot in a room that smells like gas. If you smell rotten eggs, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before calling us. For an electric heater, do not touch a wet unit until the breaker is off.

Why Lodi water is hard on tanks

The Passaic Valley Water Commission's 2026 report lists hardness between 106 and 242 ppm at the Little Falls plant, and 42 ppm in the Wanaque water blended in for most customers. Hardness in the upper range builds a layer of scale on the bottom of a tank heater and on electric elements. The scale insulates the water from the flame or element, so the heater works harder, rumbles and fails sooner.

A yearly flush removes loose sediment before it hardens, and the anode rod, which sacrifices itself to protect the tank, is worth checking every few years. Skipping both is the most common reason a heater in this area dies earlier than its rating.

How the diagnosis works

The plumber starts with the unit's age and fuel type, then moves through a short sequence:

  1. Check the water, gas and power supplies and the safety devices.
  2. Measure element resistance or thermocouple output to find the failed part.
  3. Look at the tank for rust, seepage and a spent anode rod.
  4. Explain whether repair or replacement makes more sense, with the cost of each.

A repair on a young tank is usually the right call. A leaking tank, or an older unit with scale and multiple faults, is usually better replaced. The water heater replacement page explains how that decision is made.

What decides the cost of a water heater repair

Part
A thermocouple or element is a small part. A gas control valve or a tank is not.
Age
Parts for older heaters can be harder to find, and a repair on a failing tank may not last.
Access
A heater in an open basement is simple to work on. One in a tight closet adds time.
Fuel
Gas repairs involve combustion and venting checks that electric repairs do not.

You get the price for the repair and, when relevant, the price for replacement before work begins, so you can choose.

Water heater repair questions

Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Lodi?

Yes. We answer 24/7, and a leaking tank or a no hot water call in winter is treated as a same-day job. The plumber gives you a clear price before starting work, at any hour.

Is it worth repairing an older water heater?

It depends on the fault, not only the age. A new thermostat or element is a modest repair on a heater that is otherwise sound. A leaking tank, heavy scale and repeated failures point to replacement.

Why does my water heater make a popping noise?

Water trapped under a layer of scale boils and bubbles up through it. Lodi's hard water makes this common. Flushing early can clear it, and a heater that has scaled for years may need replacement.

Can you repair a tankless water heater?

Yes. Error codes, scale buildup and flow sensor faults are the usual causes, and the tankless water heater page covers descaling and repair.

What if the water heater is in a rental?

Call us with the address and the landlord's permission if needed. We work in houses, apartments and small commercial spaces.
