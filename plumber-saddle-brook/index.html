Who supplies your water in Saddle Brook

Saddle Brook is the one town on our list that runs its own water utility. The Saddle Brook Water Department, public water system ID NJ0257001, buys all of its drinking water from other systems, including Veolia Water New Jersey Hackensack. Its 2026 water quality report covers 2025 data.

That report lists a lead 90th percentile of 1.12 ppb from 61 samples taken between July and December 2025, and one of the 61 samples exceeded the 15 ppb action level. The report says lead in drinking water is primarily from service lines and home plumbing. The department posts a service line inventory on the township website, and residents can call it at 201-587-2905 about service line materials or lead testing.

For us, that means a few practical things. If a pipe inside your house is old, a plumber can tell you what it is made of, and our repiping page explains the options. If you want to know about the pipe between the street and your meter, the township's department is the one to ask.

Water from the Saddle River

The river that gave the township its name also floods it. One resident said his basement took on water for the first time in 15 years, and added that there is no place for the water to go.

Before that, Hurricane Floyd in September 1999 was the storm that led the township to create its emergency alert station, SBC-TV. Does the Saddle Brook Water Department handle plumbing inside my house?

No. The department supplies the water and maintains the system up to your service connection. The pipes inside the house are your responsibility, and that is where we work.

Can you check whether I have a lead service line?
